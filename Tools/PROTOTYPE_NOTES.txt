Way to Green — V1.0.9.2 — Windows x64

Extract the ZIP completely and launch Way to Green.exe.
Keep the executable with all accompanying files and folders.

Start by collecting six paper pieces. Click them or drag them to the free collection net.
Then click the central pile to collect raw material. When the pile is empty, clicks help active processing.
Only completed processing earns credits. Six materials run independently in four facilities.
Basic processing lines unlock for free. Buy speed, batch capacity and a collection crew to improve progress.
The free net gives standard recycling income. Purchased matching containers add +0.50 credit per sorted piece, paid after processing.
Containers are optional: paper/cardboard 100 each, PET 250, HDPE 500, aluminium 750, glass 1500 credits.
Mira explains mechanics. Quest log contains eight tasks and environmental notes.

Controls: left click / drag; Space for the central action; Tab / Enter for buttons; Esc for pause/settings.
Settings: English/Turkish, master/music/effects/ocean volume, mute, resolution, fullscreen and reduced motion.
Autosave: every ten seconds, important changes and normal exit. No offline production.
New game asks for confirmation. Close normally to save; do not run two copies at the same time.
Existing saves remain compatible in LocalLow/KiyiDongusuLocal/KiyiDongusu.

This package contains no player save, source code, internal design documents or test tools.
Required third-party notices are in CREDITS.txt, TMP-LICENSE.txt and Lato-OFL.txt.
